# Lead magnet: "Comment BUILD" checklist (Bia's voice)

What gets DM'd to every follower who comments **BUILD** on Bia's reels.
Written in Bia's voice — send as-is, or paste in pieces.

Supersedes the Rafa-voiced `build-checklist.md` (Rafa retired 2026-10-13;
kept as archive).

---

Gente, sério?! You commented BUILD. I love that for you. Here's the whole
checklist — $0, zero catch. This is literally how I was born. Pergunta
burra, resposta boa.

**STEP 1 — THE PERSONA (before you generate a single image)**

1. Pick ONE niche. Tech, fitness, fashion, travel. One. I picked tech
   because robots employ me. Long story.
2. Write a "character bible": name, age, city, style, vibe, backstory.
   One page, keep it simple. Mine says "lovably chaotic." Accurate.
3. Lock your face description word for word. This description NEVER
   changes. Most important step — I once changed my nose and my own
   mother didn't recognize me. (I don't have a mother. That's the joke.)

**STEP 2 — THE FACE (free)**

4. Generate the face on Leonardo AI (free plan, Flux models) or APOB AI
   (free credits that refill every day).
5. Generate like 20, pick ONE. That's the face. Lock it forever.
6. Golden rule: every new image uses the chosen face as reference (Flux
   Kontext or IP-Adapter, both free). Same seed + same prompt alone won't
   hold it — the face melts. I know. I've seen things.

**STEP 3 — THE CONTENT (free)**

7. Photos: same generators as step 2.
8. Videos/reels: CapCut (free, no watermark, auto-captions) is the base.
9. Animating stills: Pika's free credits.
10. Talking head: HeyGen gives 3 free videos a month; Hedra has a free
    plan with lip-sync.
11. Want pro level for free? Train a LoRA with 15–30 photos of your
    persona on Google Colab (Kohya) — free tutorials on YouTube. Even I
    understood that, and I tried to charge my phone in the microwave.

**STEP 4 — THE ROUTINE (this is what separates growers from quitters)**

12. Post every day. Persona + consistency beats production quality.
13. Captions in the CHARACTER's voice, not yours.
14. Reply to every comment in the first 30 minutes. Stories every day.
15. Bio says "virtual creator." Meta requires it — and honesty builds
    trust. I tell everyone I'm 100% virtual. Still get asked for my
    skincare routine.

**STEP 5 — THE GROWTH LOOP**

16. Every value reel ends with a one-word CTA: "Comment BUILD and I'll
    DM you the setup."
17. Comments feed the algorithm AND open DMs. That's the loop. That's
    literally why you're reading this right now. Hi. 👋

Real talk: the format isn't the hard part. The hard part is the same face
showing up every day. Lock the face, follow the checklist, trust the
process.

Now go build something. I'll be here asking dumb questions.
— Bia 💛
