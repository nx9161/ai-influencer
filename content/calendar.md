## Posting-time experiment

Nobody knows Bia's best hour yet — no account data exists. Until it does,
rotate comparable posts through **9am, 1pm, and 7pm Eastern** and compare
saves, shares, reach, and follows over several weeks. These are test windows,
not established best times. Lock in the winner once the data speaks.

## Caption rules (Bia's voice)

- Short. Playful. Chaotic good. One idea per caption.
- Minimal captions win (abu.shalab / jean_philanthrope study): 1–5 words
  + emoji is often enough. Let the video do the work.
- Rotate the catchphrases: "Gente, sério?!" / "Pergunta burra, resposta boa."
- Every value reel ends with a soft CTA — assets, not gates
  ("checklist in the comments", "follow for the next one").
- End with a question when you want comments.
- Portuguese flavor is the brand; keep it readable for English followers.
- Always honest: wins AND failures, dumb questions AND real answers.
- Bio discloses from day one: 100% virtual, 0% juízo.
